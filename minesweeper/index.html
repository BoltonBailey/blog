Probability Minesweeper

W H Mines
For every click after the first, the game computes the probability that the cell is safe given everything you can see (and the total mine count), multiplies your health by it, and then re-draws a random mine layout that is consistent with the visible numbers and with your clicked cell being safe. Your health is therefore the probability that all your guesses so far would have succeeded, if the game were being played as normal.
